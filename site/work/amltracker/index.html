Case study

AMLtracker

Live

A free calendar of upcoming UK Part-66 exam sittings, for people studying for the aircraft maintenance licence. No account is needed. This page does not claim that I hold that licence.

Open the calendar

Problem

ShippedA free calendar of upcoming UK Part-66 exam sittings, for people studying for the aircraft maintenance licence. Dates come from the UK CAA and Part-147 centres, and no account is needed.

Sitting dates are published by the UK CAA and by Part-147 centres, on separate pages. AMLtracker collects them into one calendar so a reader does not have to hunt each provider. The public page says it is for people booking B1, B2 and Category A module exams. That is the calendar's audience, not a description of me.

What is published

ShippedEvery date on the live calendar links to its source.

The public page says the dates are copied from public web pages and PDFs, that they can change or fill up, and that a reader should confirm with the provider before booking or travelling. It also says AMLtracker is independent of the CAA and of any training organisation.

The raw list is linked from the calendar at /data/sittings.json. Filters on the page need JavaScript. The list itself is in the HTML.

Stack

No public source repository for AMLtracker is linked from this site, so no framework list is stated here. The calendar is the public page at amltracker.pages.dev.

What I owned

The home page says each product under Drew & Crew, including AMLtracker, was directed by me: I set the objective, supplied the context and reviewed the result. A finer split between my decisions and AI-assisted implementation is not written down for AMLtracker the way it is for TextBackCaller and PitchReach. It is not invented here.

Evidence

ShippedThe public calendar, updated 9 November 2026, stated 180 sittings from public sources. The same page says the dates can change, and that entries marked check are guide dates or might be wrong.

LimitThis portfolio does not run a live sittings counter. Figures on the public calendar can change.

180 is a sentence the public page printed when it was updated on 9 November 2026. It is not a counter on this portfolio, and it will go stale when the calendar changes. Entries the page marks "check" are, in its own words, guide dates or might be wrong.

Source: amltracker.pages.dev, read 15 November 2026.
