Case study

TextBackCaller

Live

A missed call becomes one text, from the business's own number, unless a guard says no. A person still decides when setup fails, and before a release.

Live product Simulated demo Engineering note

Problem

UK small businesses cannot always reach the phone. The public site says the caller then has little more than a number and a time, and may try another business. The product keeps the number the customers already call.

ShippedFor UK small businesses.

ShippedCustomers keep their existing business number.

Architecture

Two paths. Both are on the home page in full. This is the short form.

Once, at signup

  1. The customer pays by card, through Stripe Checkout.
  2. A signed payment webhook starts provisioning on a Cloudflare Worker.
  3. A UK mobile number is bought through the Twilio API.
  4. A setup text and a setup email go out, through Twilio SMS and Resend.
  5. If setup fails, or a trial is refused, the owner is alerted and a person decides.

Every missed call

  1. The phone network diverts the missed call to the customer's TextBackCaller number.
  2. A signed webhook arrives. The call is rejected as busy at once.
  3. Guards are checked. One text goes to the caller from the business's own number, or none does.

ShippedOne text per caller per 24 hours, UK mobile numbers only, and the owner's own calls are skipped.

ShippedSignup, payment, number provisioning and setup messages run automatically, with no manual step between a paid signup and a working service.

ShippedEach service is called through its API: Twilio for voice and SMS, Stripe for checkout and billing, Resend for email, D1 for storage.

Stack

ShippedBuilt on Cloudflare Workers, D1, Twilio Voice and SMS, Stripe Checkout and Billing, and Resend.

ShippedWritten in JavaScript on the Cloudflare Workers runtime, with SQL in D1 for data, and HTML, CSS and browser JavaScript for the pages.

What I owned

ShippedBuilt as AI-assisted automation: AI workers implement each staged release and call tools through APIs and connectors. Reviewed the evidence and approved.

I direct the product and approve the releases. AI workers implement each staged release and call the service APIs. That is assistance with the build, not a claim that a model runs the missed-call decision on the live path. The live path described here is webhooks, guards and the provider APIs.

The public site also says an optional AI receptionist, which would answer calls and take bookings, is coming soon. It is not described here as shipped.

Where a person decides

Human gateEvery release needs approval. Builds are staged, tested, backed up and given a rollback point first.

Human gateNew features ship switched off. They are switched on in a separate, approved change, and a kill switch turns each one off again.

Human gateWhen automatic setup fails or a trial is refused, the owner is alerted so a person decides what happens next.

ShippedRelease controls: signed webhooks, duplicate-send prevention, opt-outs, usage caps and automated tests.
